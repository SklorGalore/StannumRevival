![Power flow overview of a 300-bus case: convergence, totals, voltage profile and branch loading](../../assets/screens/overview-charts.png)

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Power flow" icon="rocket">
    Newton and decoupled AC flow with reactive limits, remote voltage
    regulation, switched shunts, tap changers, phase shifters, FACTS devices,
    area interchange and detailed HVDC: two-terminal LCC, VSC, multi-terminal
    DC and meshed VSC grids. Linear DC flow, sensitivities, PTDF and LODF,
    and N-1 contingency analysis.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Diagram" icon="pencil">
    Generate one-lines from the case around any bus, with solved flows and
    voltages on them, or draw and export your own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Topology" icon="puzzle">
    Islands, bridges, cut buses and fewest-hop paths, drawn as layers on the
    network graph and the one-line, with tables to export.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Records" icon="list-format">
    Every record of the case as a paged table, fast on 80,000-bus cases, to
    sort, filter and edit. Edits carry into PSS/E RAW 33 and 35 exports.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Case diff" icon="document">
    Exact, record-by-record comparison of two RAW cases, with no floating-point
    tolerance to hide a change.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## Where to start

- New to Stannum? Start with [What Stannum is](/StannumRevival/start/introduction/) and [Open a case](/StannumRevival/start/open-a-case/).
- Looking for a button or shortcut? The [user guide](/StannumRevival/guide/shell/) walks through every module.
- Want to know what the solver does with your data? The [technical manual](/StannumRevival/manual/architecture/) gives the equations, control logic and what is not modelled.
- Working on Stannum itself? The [developer guide](/StannumRevival/develop/overview/) explains the codebase, the command registry, and how to build, test and add to it.
