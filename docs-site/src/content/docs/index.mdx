![Power flow overview of a 300-bus case: convergence, totals, voltage profile and branch loading](../../assets/screens/overview-charts.png)

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Power flow" icon="rocket">
    Sparse Newton–Raphson AC flow with reactive limits, remote voltage
    regulation, switched shunts, tap changers and detailed HVDC: two-terminal
    LCC, VSC and multi-terminal DC. Linear DC flow, sensitivities, PTDF and
    LODF.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Diagram" icon="pencil">
    Generate one-lines from the case around any bus, with solved flows and
    voltages on them, or draw and export your own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Topology" icon="puzzle">
    Islands, bridges, cut buses and fewest-hop paths, drawn as layers on the
    network graph and the one-line, with tables to export.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Case diff" icon="document">
    Exact, record-by-record comparison of two RAW cases, with no floating-point
    tolerance to hide a change.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
